Legacy work

January 12, 2026

While working in hospice I witnessed that many people would like to spend more time on leaving a legacy for the ones they leave behind. How that was done depended on the person’s wishes. These are some different ways people have chosen to do legacy work:

  • write down stories that were never shared before
  • write letters to loved ones
  • create videos with personal questions and messages
  • create art pieces

Life Reconnect can help you find the right legacy project for your journey and offer support in telling parts of your stories you want your loved ones to remember and pass on.

Please contact us if you would like to know more.
