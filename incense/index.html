Incense

As we follow our path through life, we encounter challenges. I cannot express how important it is to practice self compassion and self care. Although I did not realize it, for years I have been using incense as self care. Whenever I would light and smell incense to me it felt like a breath of fresh air and a ‘re-set’. It allowed me to come back into the now instead of lingering in the past or fretting about the future as we all tend to do. This does not have to be your self care, there are many people that do not enjoy incense at all, but find their self care in other forms.

After close to 20 years of using this incense myself, I have decided to import Raj Sat Prem incense so as to make it available to people in Canada. The incense is hand rolled in India by monks during Bhakti yoga. Bhakti Yoga is the yoga practice of love and devotion to the divine which respects and cares for all living beings and nature.

There are 29 different blends. All are made of 100% organic material. The blends have been carefully prepared according to recipes from ancient Vedic scriptures. Aromatic roots, honey, wild herbs & flowers, gums, resins and quality oils have been used.
