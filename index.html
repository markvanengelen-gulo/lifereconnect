Grief, Trauma and Parent Counselling, in the Kootenays, British Columbia, Canada

Life Reconnect offers counselling, coaching and support for people experiencing grief, trauma and facing end of life. Both individual and group support is offered. Together we will find strategies that target memories and emotions that are stored on the different levels (physical, mental, emotional and spiritual).

Marije van Engelen, RSW, BSW, CTRC, CGSC, has experience in supporting children, adults and families as they navigate different emotions that surface in their grief. Marije has experience in both volunteering and working in hospice.

Marije supports people in reconnecting to self, others and life. She works from a holistic, culturally sensitive approach, incorporating physical, mental, emotional and spiritual (what gives meaning and purpose in life) levels using strategies and ancient healing methods such as mindfulness, therapeutic art and ritual.

The services offered include

  • Grief Counselling
  • Trauma Recovery Counselling
  • End of Life coaching
  • Legacy Projects
  • Parent counselling and workshops

Life Reconnect is based in the Kootenays, British Columbia, Canada. Contact us for more information.
